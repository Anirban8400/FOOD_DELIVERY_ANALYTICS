# 🍽️ Food Delivery Analytics  
### Turning Raw Data into Business Decisions  
**Python • SQL • Power BI | End-to-End Analytics Project**

---

## 🚀 Why This Project?

Food delivery platforms operate in a highly competitive environment where **speed, pricing, and customer experience** directly impact retention and growth.

This project simulates a **real-world FoodTech analytics use case**, where raw restaurant data is transformed into **actionable business insights** that can support:
- Operational efficiency
- Customer satisfaction
- City-level strategy
- Restaurant onboarding & promotion decisions

---

## 🎯 What This Project Solves

This dashboard answers key business questions such as:

- Does higher pricing actually lead to better customer ratings?  
- How critical is delivery speed to customer satisfaction?  
- Which cities and cuisines perform better operationally?  
- Which restaurants are high-quality but under-discovered?  

The focus is not just on charts, but on **decision-making**.

---

## 🧠 Analytics Approach

The project follows an end-to-end analytics workflow:

1. **Data Cleaning & Feature Engineering (Python)**  
   - Standardized pricing, ratings, and delivery metrics  
   - Created business-friendly categories (delivery speed, price bands, popularity)

2. **Analytical Logic (SQL)**  
   - Designed clean table structures  
   - Used aggregation and filtering for business questions

3. **Insight Delivery (Power BI)**  
   - KPI-driven dashboard design  
   - Page-wise storytelling from overview to strategy  
   - Metrics documented for transparency and trust

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Stack

- **Python** → Pandas, NumPy (EDA & feature engineering)  
- **SQL** → Structured analysis & logic building  
- **Power BI** → KPIs, DAX measures, business storytelling  

---

## 📊 Dashboard Design Philosophy

The dashboard is intentionally structured to guide stakeholders:

- **Executive Overview** → Platform health at a glance  
- **Customer & Pricing Insights** → Experience over assumptions  
- **Delivery & Operations** → SLA adherence & bottlenecks  
- **Restaurant Strategy** → Promote, fix, or deprioritize decisions  

Each page answers a **specific business question**.

---

## 🔑 High-Impact Business Insights

- Customer satisfaction depends more on **delivery experience** than pricing  
- Faster delivery consistently leads to **higher ratings**  
- Operational performance varies significantly across cities  
- High-quality restaurants can remain **under-utilized without visibility**  
- Cuisine performance differs in both popularity and customer experience
